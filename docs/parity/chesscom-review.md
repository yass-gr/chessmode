# Game review behavior spec

The reference is Chess.com's desktop web review. Match the order of actions, feedback, and transitions. Use the Chessmode design system, original coach and copy, and the local engine and scoring model in [PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md). The mobile help article corroborates interactions that should also work on touch.

## Evidence and limits

On 2026-11-25, fetched the public [Analysis page](https://www.chess.com/analysis) and a [master-game analysis page](https://www.chess.com/analysis/game/master/100553) with curl, followed their module imports and stylesheet dependencies, and formatted the relevant bundles with Prettier. The web build was `2026.11.12`. The inventory describes branches in the shipped client, including settings and feature-gated variants. It does not establish which experiments a particular account receives.

- **UI** covers the [analysis entry](https://www.chess.com/r2/client-packages/analysis/2026.11.12/analysis.js), [review tab](https://www.chess.com/r2/client-packages/analysis/2026.11.12/tab-review.fad24eb7.BwXvcRqqEm.chunk.js), and [review styles](https://www.chess.com/r2/client-packages/analysis/2026.11.12/tab-review.D_BWUnqFZ_.css). Look for `overview-view`, `game-overview`, `tallies`, `game-arc`, `move-by-move`, and `takeaways`.
- **Coach** covers `move-feedback`, `animated-bot-speech`, and `flow-buttons` in the [coach chunk](https://www.chess.com/r2/client-packages/analysis/2026.11.12/shared.9a16f5c6.C-9L1qIfpv.chunk.js) and the review, retry, explanation, and playback handlers in the [shared chunk](https://www.chess.com/r2/client-packages/analysis/2026.11.12/shared.eager.DEas_m121x.chunk.js).
- **Bar** covers the [evaluation component](https://www.chess.com/r2/client-packages/analysis/2026.11.12/evaluation-bar.dec2c45b.PATlYy9oqo.chunk.js), its [formatting and scaling helpers](https://www.chess.com/r2/client-packages/analysis/2026.11.12/shared.d33dce8b.Vh7GXL7-EG.chunk.js), [bar styles](https://www.chess.com/r2/client-packages/analysis/2026.11.12/evaluation-bar.BopZJgJ5Vr.css), and [global motion tokens](https://www.chess.com/bundles/app/vite/main-global-styles.BNrHR8gNoD.css).
- **Help** means the official [web guide](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8584089-how-does-game-review-work), [app guide](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/10328363-how-do-i-use-game-review-on-the-app), [classifications](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8572705-how-are-moves-classified-what-is-a-blunder-or-brilliant-etc), [accuracy](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8708970-how-is-accuracy-in-analysis-determined), and [single-game rating](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/10773754-how-is-game-rating-calculated-in-game-review).

The public client consumes classifications, per-side accuracy and rating, and supplied coach lines. Exact reference rating coefficients, accuracy aggregation, special-label thresholds and tie-breaking, key-moment ranking, and retry acceptance bands were not established. The inventory gives observed presentation rules and documented scoring bands. Proposed choices for Chessmode are in the acceptance criteria.

### Move classification rules and boundaries

The documented model measures the mover's dropped expected points on a 0–1 scale using engine evaluation and rating. A drop matters more in some positions than others. The public table gives bands sharing endpoints. Endpoint ownership, model parameters and account-dependent special thresholds are unverified. Chessmode's observed endpoints are half-open below, subject to its floating-point tolerance. [Reference thresholds and special cases](https://support.chess.com/en/articles/8572705-how-are-moves-classified-what-is-a-blunder-or-brilliant-etc)

| Class | Reference rule in Chessmode terminology | Chessmode status and rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| best | 0 loss, engine first choice | **Done.** Engine first choice gets zero loss. Other moves with effectively zero measured loss also qualify. |
| strong | 0–.02 | **Done.** Positive loss below .02. |
| good | .02–.05 | **Done.** .02 to below .05. |
| loose (?!) | .05–.10 | **Done.** .05 to below .10. |
| mistake (?) | .10–.20 | **Done.** .10 to below .20. |
| blunder (??) | .20–1 | **Done.** At least .20 absent a special class. |
| book | Established opening move | **Done.** Opening lookup applies until the first position leaves the book. |
| only | Chessmode reserves this for a position with one legal move | **Done.** Book takes precedence, then a single legal move. |
| find (!!) | Sound sacrifice on a top or near-top move, with an acceptable result and a meaningful need to find it. Rating affects generosity. | **Partial.** Loss ≤ .02, resulting expected points ≥ .50, best expected points < .97, and projected material sacrifice ≥ 2. Rating-independent. |
| sharp (!) | Outcome-changing or uniquely good move. Rating can affect whether the position is considered winning, equal or losing. | **Partial.** First engine choice, gap to second ≥ .10, second < .90, and excludes immediate recaptures. |
| missed | Failing to convert a chance offered by the opponent, often leaving an equal or worse position. | **Partial.** Previous loss ≥ .10 or a forced mate was available, and current loss ≥ .05. A loss ≥ .20 that leaves expected points below .50 takes precedence. |

All rows use [classify.ts](../../src/lib/review/classify.ts#L20) and [classify tests](../../src/lib/review/classify.test.ts). The base-band **Done** status covers grouping and boundaries. Rating calibration remains unverified. For a move occurring within MultiPV, Chessmode compares candidates evaluated at the same root. Otherwise it compares the best root score with the played position's score. The local logistic model does not use ratings. [Analysis](../../src/lib/review/analyse.ts#L112), [classify](../../src/lib/review/classify.ts#L33).

## 4. Best move, follow-up and retry

| Behavior | Chessmode status |
| --- | --- |
| Best-move comparison backs up to the decision, plays the recommended alternative, and shows that position's evaluation and explanation. Return restores the original played ply. When a follow-up belongs to the played move, start after it. When it starts with an alternative, branch from before the played move and show why it works. The coach action describes the available theme, such as a fork or mate. **Coach, Help** | **Partial.** One best PV opens at the prior position. No separate best-move preview, played-move reply, or theme-specific continuation. The route drops contextual overlays and shows a single root score throughout. | Follow-ups support autoplay, manual ply stepping and selecting a step in the line. Autoplay defaults on with **1,000 ms** between plies. An ordinary continuation begins with an immediate first step after setup, then ticks. An alternate candidate can rewind and replay with a **1,000 ms** pause. The separate explanation path disables autoplay and can pair each step with its own speech and score. Hide restores the anchor and cancels playback. **UI, Coach, Help** | **Partial.** Manual step buttons, SAN selection, keyboard and return exist. No autoplay, per-step coach overlay, or score updates. | Retry is offered at an eligible reviewed move. It clears annotations and takes the player through a brief rewind into the position before the decision. ## 5. Implementation batches

Each batch owns a disjoint set of write paths. A later batch can consume exports from another batch. Batch F owns route integration, including wiring settings and consumers to the other batches. S = one small component or pure helper, M = multiple states and fixtures, L = new review behavior with several branches. The timing references above inform these targets. Use Chessmode styling and terminology throughout.

### Batch A · Analysis data

- **RV-01 · Rating and outcome context (M).** Show estimated performance as a single-game number with unavailable states. Acceptance: keep Lichess-style accuracy and established base loss boundaries, add explicit missing-data and short-sample fixtures, and evaluate known find, sharp and missed cases before changing local heuristics. Existing cached reviews remain readable, and any derivation change invalidates stale results. Files: `src/lib/review/{accuracy,classify,analyse,types}.ts` and adjacent tests.
- **RV-02 · Explain the played and recommended moves (L).** Give the coach independently derived explanations and legal continuations for both branches. Acceptance: fixtures cover mate, material gain, fork, opening exit and quiet moves. Each step carries valid board context, uses an evaluation only if known, and supports plain fallback text. Store branch data so a cached review works offline. Files: `src/lib/review/{explain,material,analyse,types}.ts` and adjacent tests.

### Batch B · Review session and key moments

- **RV-03 · Guided key moments (M).** Advance through a concise set for the chosen side or both sides. Acceptance: stable chronological order, no duplicates, opening anchor when known, and an explicit end state on the last move. Imported games with unknown player color allow selecting either side or both. Provide a pure selection contract for the route. Files: `src/lib/review/moments.ts` and its tests.
- **RV-04 · Branch navigation and autoplay (M).** Preview an alternative and explore the played or best continuation. Acceptance: manual and automatic stepping share one cursor, default interval is 1,000 ms, and hide, selected-ply change or route exit invalidates pending steps. Opening a branch and returning restores the exact mainline anchor. Files: `src/lib/review/session.ts` and its tests.
- **RV-05 · Retry this decision (L).** Retry the active position with a hint, clear feedback and a way to continue. Acceptance: choice of retrying the current position or running the existing queue, pre-move position reached before input is enabled, hint cycles from origin square to full arrow to clear, legal promotions work, equivalent moves within the current tolerance remain accepted, and late engine answers after exit have no effect. Cache hits and restricted searches produce the same feedback shape, with the submitted move's score when known. Files: `src/lib/review/session.ts`, `src/lib/engine/types.ts` and `src/lib/engine/stockfish.ts` if required for restricted search, and adjacent tests.

### Batch C · Graph and evaluation

- **RV-06 · Graph selection and position-aware bar (M).** Make both indicators track the displayed position. Acceptance: click or tap an evaluated ply, move keyboard focus predictably, distinguish hover from selection, center equal positions, saturate large scores, show mate and results distinctly, and maintain correct orientation when flipped. Apply the reference scale and fill timing to the standard bar. Both widgets show a neutral or unavailable state when score data is missing. Files: `src/lib/components/review/EvalGraph.svelte`, new `EvalBar.svelte` and optional pure helpers under `src/lib/components/review/`.

### Batch D · Overview and coach

- **RV-07 · Loading and finished overview (M).** Give completed analysis a distinct overview with coach verdict, graph, side-by-side accuracy and integer ratings. Acceptance: placeholders never show fabricated numbers, rating unavailable states have a reason, compact tallies expand with 150 ms size and 100 ms opacity transitions, and each nonzero tally offers a side-specific jump to its first matching ply. Files: `src/lib/components/review/Summary.svelte`, new `Overview.svelte` and `ReviewLoading.svelte`.
- **RV-08 · Coach presentation (M).** Pair the crowned horse coach with selected-move text and contextual actions. Acceptance: loading, verdict, mainline, follow-up, retry and completion have coherent coach states. After rapid navigation, only the current text and reaction remain. Coach visibility and voice are independent, text stays available, and reduced-motion users see immediate text. Files: new `src/lib/components/review/Coach.svelte`, `src/lib/components/review/Label.svelte`, `static/review/coach/` and `static/review/icons/`.

### Batch E · Sound cues

- **RV-09 · Play review cues at the right event (M).** Add original board and retry feedback sounds, with independently muteable narration if enabled. Acceptance: play one board cue per animated move, use check/capture/promotion/castle precedence, avoid bursts on direct jumps or setup, and cancel stale voice on navigation. The find cue plays once when landing on an eligible moment. Respect saved sound settings and user-gesture requirements. Files: `src/lib/sound.ts`, new `src/lib/review/review-audio.ts`, optional assets in `static/review/audio/`.

### Batch F · Route and board integration

- **RV-10 · Wire the full review sequence (L).** Open into loading or cached overview, start the coached pass, advance through key moments, return from variations and retry, and conclude at the final position. Acceptance: at 390×844 and 1440×900 the board and current action stay reachable, graph/list/board stay in sync, 200 ms guided replay ticks cancel on navigation, and mascot reacts only for the settled selection. Persist review preferences for perspective, overlays, autoplay and voice through existing settings. Confirm a checkmate PGN, a Black-side game, a short game, a promotion, and cached reopen. Keep the existing repertoire learn/drill link reachable. Files: `src/routes/review/[id]/+page.svelte`, `src/lib/components/Board.svelte`, with preference calls through the existing `db.ts` API.
